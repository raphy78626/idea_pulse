## Pain & evidence
Small companies and newsletter writers who send mail from their own domain (for example @yourstartup.in) are finding that Outlook.com silently drops their messages. There is often no bounce and no spam folder notice—the email just vanishes. A common cause is a missing DMARC record, which is a DNS entry that proves to email providers that the sender is legitimate. DNS is the internet’s phonebook that maps domain names to servers.

Evidence: a Hacker News post about Outlook rejecting domains without DMARC earned 279 points and drew comments from confused senders. Google and Yahoo also tightened sender rules in 2024, so awareness is rising. Still, most small domain owners do not know DMARC exists, which means the pain is real but hidden.
