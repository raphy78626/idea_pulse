## Verdict
SKIP | 3/10  
One-line: A joke app with a brief viral spike, no paying users, and no defense against clones; only ship if you can finish it in two days and treat it as a lottery ticket.

## Pain & evidence
The dev.to post with 46 likes shows curiosity, not demand. No one asked to pay. The real pain is mild boredom and the desire for a funny screenshot to share. That is weak pain. People already roast each other for free on Reddit and with free artificial intelligence chat. You cannot sell painkillers for a hangnail.

## Target buyer
Who pays: almost no one. The user is a teenager or tech worker who wants a shareable roast card. They hang out on Twitter/X, Instagram meme pages, Reddit (r/india, r/programmerhumor), and dev.to. Since they will not pay, your real customer would be an advertiser or sponsor, which a solo micro-product rarely lands.

## Competition & wedge

| Alternative | What it does | Why it wins | Your wedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free chat (Claude, ChatGPT) | Roasts anything typed | Free, unlimited, smarter | None |
| Reddit roast.me | Humans roast your post | Viral, community feel | None |
| Other roast websites | Generates roast text | Already on Google | One narrow target plus image card |

Wedge: roast one narrow thing, such as a LinkedIn headline, turn it into an image, and require no signup. Everything else is too broad or too human.

## Monetization
Price: ₹49 ($0.60) for a bundle of five custom roast images; ₹99 ($1.20) for “brutal mode” and custom targets.  
Expected first-month revenue: ₹0 to ₹3,000 ($0 to $35). Most traffic will be free users. Do not quit your day job.

## Distribution
Exact 3 channels:

1. Twitter/X: post roast images with #RoastMyProfile; reply to tech accounts with over 10k followers.
2. Reddit: share in r/india and r/programmerhumor; put the link only in comments after adding value.
3. WhatsApp and Telegram: make cards easy to forward; seed them in college and developer groups.

## Risks & kill criteria
Risks: artificial intelligence cost per roast eats margins; an offensive roast gets you banned; clones appear in a week; users share images but never return.

Kill criteria: fewer than 50 users in 14 days; zero revenue after 30 days; AI cost higher than income for seven days straight.

## 7-day MVP plan
Day 1: Choose one target, for example LinkedIn headlines. Build a one-page site on Vercel and connect it to Claude for roast text.
Day 2: Add an input box and generate the roast. Turn the text into a shareable image card. No login.
Day 3: Deploy with a custom domain. Add a simple visitor counter. Test with 10 friends on WhatsApp.
Day 4: Add five card styles and a “Post to X” button. Make the image the whole product.
Day 5: Launch on dev.to, Twitter, and Reddit. Message 20 meme pages.
Day 6: Add a Razorpay payment link for ₹49 for a five-roast bundle. Keep the free version limited to one roast per day.
Day 7: Count users, shares, and revenue. If below kill criteria, stop or pivot to a different joke.

## Final call
Do not build this as a business. Build it as a 48-hour joke if you want Twitter followers, then move on.
