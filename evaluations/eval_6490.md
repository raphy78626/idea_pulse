# Deep Evaluation — ClarifierAI for Chrome

*Source: Product Hunt · Use AI for writing & translating in your Chrome · evaluated 2026-11-08 14:55 · model: nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl:free*

---

## Verdict (SKIP + 3/10)
Too many free tools already fix writing and translate inside Chrome; a solo developer in India will struggle to get paying users before money runs out.

## Pain & evidence
People copy text from LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or email, paste it into a translator or AI chat, fix it, then copy it back. It wastes time. Evidence: Product Hunt lists more than 20 similar “AI writing assistant” extensions released in the last year; most have under 50 reviews and no visible payment success.

## Target buyer (who pays, where they hang out)
| Buyer | Where they hang out | Pays? |
| Indian freelancers and students writing English as a second language | WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn, Telegram, Reddit r/india | Yes, if under ₹299/month |
| Small shop owners writing product captions | Instagram, WhatsApp Business | Maybe, one-time |

## Competition & wedge
| Alternative | What it does | Weakness | Our wedge |
| Grammarly | Fixes grammar and tone | Costly, ignores Indian English | Built for Hinglish and Indian languages |
| DeepL | Translates text | Few Indian languages, no writing fix | One-click fix plus translate in one button |
| QuillBot | Rewrites sentences | Slow, clunky extension | Works inside WhatsApp Web and LinkedIn |

## Monetization
| Plan | INR | USD | Limit |
| Free | ₹0 | $0 | 10 fixes per day |
| Monthly | ₹199 | $2.40 | Unlimited |
| Yearly | ₹1,499 | $18 | Unlimited |

Expected first-month revenue: ₹0 to ₹20,000 ($0 to $240). Realistic: under ₹3,000 ($36).

## Distribution (exact 3 channels)
1. Post in 30 Indian freelancer WhatsApp and Telegram groups offering free fixes in exchange for feedback.
2. Daily LinkedIn posts by the founder showing “before/after” of broken English messages.
3. Product Hunt launch plus Chrome Web Store description using words like “Hindi to English translator.”

## Risks & kill-criteria
- Google can remove extensions without warning.
- AI usage cost per user can exceed subscription price.
- Users install once and never return.
Kill criteria: fewer than 100 installs or zero paying users after 30 days.

## 7-day MVP plan
| Day | Task | Output |
| 1 | Choose one writing task (LinkedIn posts) and one translation pair (English to Hindi) | Decision doc |
| 2 | Build right-click menu that sends selected text to an AI programming interface | Working extension |
| 3 | Add a popup to show fixed text and let user copy it | Usable first version |
| 4 | Write Chrome Web Store page, privacy policy, and screenshots | Store listing ready |
| 5 | Share with 20 WhatsApp groups; collect 10 user comments | Feedback list |
| 6 | Add Razorpay or Gumroad payment link for ₹199/month | Payment live |
| 7 | Launch on Product Hunt and LinkedIn; track installs daily | Launch complete |

## Final call
Skip this idea unless the developer can own a tiny niche such as Indian-language WhatsApp correction. Otherwise, build a narrower micro-tool instead.
