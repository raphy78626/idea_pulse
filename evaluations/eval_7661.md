## Verdict
**WATCH | 4/10**  
Do not sell yet. The app earns too little for a meaningful payout, costs nothing to run, and a quick sale would net only a few months of fees. Keep it while you add one paid option.

## Pain & evidence
You need semester fees now, but the asset is small. It makes about $2.2 per day via AdMob (Google’s ad network), or roughly ₹5,500 per month, with zero server cost. Buyers will pay roughly 6 to 12 times monthly profit for a no-backend ad app, which puts a sale price around ₹30,000 to ₹70,000 ($360 to $840). That is not fast or large enough unless you accept a lowball offer. The only real evidence of value is 9,500 installs and 120 daily organic adds.

## Target buyer
**Who pays:** Another solo Android developer or a tiny app studio that wants an existing user base to cross-promote its own apps.  
**Where they hang out:** Flippa, Acquire.com, Twitter/X, Telegram groups for app flippers, LinkedIn, and Indian student/developer subreddits.

## Competition & wedge
| Alternative | What they do | Weakness | Your wedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Other free utility apps | Same job on Play Store | Abandoned, bloated with ads | Clean app, growing 120 installs/day |
| App-flipping sites | Buy cheap apps and resell | No real product, just traffic | Real installs, zero ad spend |
| Mobile game studios | Casual games with ads | Users leave fast, high ad cost | Utility use means longer retention |

## Monetization
Two paths, pick one:
- **Sell now:** One-time price ₹40,000 to ₹80,000 ($480 to $950), paid upfront. Expect to negotiate down.
- **Keep and grow:** Add a one-time “Remove ads” purchase at ₹49 ($0.60). With current traffic, first-month range is ₹7,000 to ₹15,000 ($85 to $180).

Do not add subscriptions; Indian students will not commit to recurring payments for a tiny utility.

## Distribution
Exact three channels:
1. **Google Play Store listing:** Rewrite the title and description around one clear benefit; add three screenshots showing the app in use.
2. **Indian student communities:** Post in subreddits like r/India and r/college, and in Telegram groups where students share tools.
3. **WhatsApp and Instagram Reels:** A 15-second video showing the problem and the app solving it.

## Risks & kill-criteria
Google can disable AdMob without warning. Organic growth can stop overnight if a better free clone appears. Revenue is tied to one ad network.  
Kill criteria: Earnings under ₹3,000 ($36) per month for 60 days, or AdMob disables your account. If either happens, sell for scrap or shut down.

## 7-day MVP plan
Day 1: Export AdMob reports and write down how to rebuild the app from scratch.  
Day 2: Add a one-time “Remove ads” in-app purchase at ₹49.  
Day 3: Update Play Store text and screenshots.  
Day 4: Post the app in three Indian student forums and two Telegram groups.  
Day 5: List the app for sale on Flippa or Acquire at ₹60,000 asking price.  
Day 6: Reply to buyers with revenue screenshots; negotiate.  
Day 7: Accept any offer above ₹40,000 or keep the app and run it for another month.

## Final call
Hold the app and add a small paid option instead of selling today; a sale now gives you too little, too late. If you still need cash this week, list it at ₹50,000 and accept the first serious offer above ₹40,000.
