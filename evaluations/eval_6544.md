## Verdict
WATCH | 4 / 10  
A mild curiosity, not a painkiller. Most site owners are not losing sleep over AI crawlers yet, so fast cash is unlikely.

## Pain & evidence
Site owners publish articles and wonder if artificial intelligence (AI) crawlers copy them. Robots.txt files tell bots what to read, but they are hard to write and harder to test. People ask on Reddit and X, “Is GPTBot reading my blog?” yet almost no one pays for a dedicated checker. Most webmasters ignore the problem or copy-paste a snippet from a forum.

Evidence signal: low. The source post has 69 upvotes—mild interest, not demand.

## Target buyer
Who pays: Independent bloggers, niche site owners, and small newsletter writers with original text they do not want scraped for free.  
Where they hang out: X (Twitter), Reddit (r/webdev, r/blogging), IndieHackers, Product Hunt, and niche Facebook groups.  
Note: An India-based solo dev should target buyers in the US or Europe; willingness to pay is much higher there.

## Competition & wedge
| Alternative | Cost | Weakness | Wedge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand-written robots.txt | Free | Easy to get wrong | Instant plain-English report |
| Cloudflare bot protection | Paid | Built for big sites | Simple, no setup, one web address |
| General SEO audit tools | Paid | Too complex and expensive | Focused only on AI crawlers |

## Monetization
Free tier: check one web address (URL) at a time.  
Paid tier: unlimited checks, weekly monitoring, PDF report.  
Price: ₹299 / month ($4) or ₹2,499 / year ($35).  
Expected first-month revenue: ₹0 to ₹15,000 ($0 to $180). Assume zero unless you already have an audience.

## Distribution
1. Launch on Product Hunt and share in IndieHackers and relevant subreddits.  
2. Cold email or direct-message fifty niche blog owners found through public site lists.  
3. Write three blog posts like “Can AI bots read my site?” and try to rank them on Google.

## Risks & kill-criteria
Risk: AI crawlers change their names often, so your list breaks.  
Risk: Cloudflare or Google launches a free built-in tool.  
Risk: free users never convert to paid.  
Kill criteria: fewer than 10 paying users after sixty days, or fewer than 500 free checks per month after thirty days.

## 7-day MVP plan
Day 1: List five AI crawlers and how they identify themselves.  
Day 2: Build a simple page where a user pastes a URL and sees “Allowed” or “Blocked” per bot.  
Day 3: Read the site’s robots.txt and show why each bot is allowed or blocked.  
Day 4: Add a Stripe payment button for unlimited checks.  
Day 5: Launch on Product Hunt and post in three communities.  
Day 6: Message twenty site owners and ask them to try it.  
Day 7: Fix the three most common complaints and decide whether to keep going.

## Final call
Ship a one-page free checker this week, but do not quit your day job. If nobody pays within two months, park it and move on.
