## Verdict
BUILD | 6/10  
Sixty paying users proves someone will pay, but we do not know what they pay for, how much, or if they will stay.

## Pain & evidence
Evidence: The founder claims 60 paid users. A Reddit post with 79 upvotes and 37 comments is weak proof; upvotes are not revenue.  
Pain: Unknown. The product could be anything from an invoice tool to an AI wrapper. Until you talk to users, you are guessing.  
Ruthless read: Sixty users could be friends, a cheap lifetime deal, or a $1-per-month habit. That is not a business yet.

## Target buyer
Who pays: Small shop owners, freelancers, or solo developers in India who need a cheap, simple tool.  
Where they hang out: WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn, niche Facebook groups, IndieHackers, Product Hunt, Reddit.

## Competition & wedge
| Alternative | Price | How you differ |
|---|---|---|
| Excel or Google Sheets | Free | You save time; no setup |
| Expensive business software | ₹5,000+ per month | You are built for one person |
| Fiverr or Upwork freelancer | ₹2,000+ per job | You charge a small monthly fee |

## Monetization
Price: ₹299–₹799 per month (about $4–$10) per user. Offer a yearly plan at 10 months' price.  
First-month revenue: ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000 (about $350 to $1,800), assuming you keep current users and add 10–40 new ones.  
Payment: Use Razorpay for India cards and UPI, and Stripe for international cards.

## Distribution
Exact three channels:
1. Direct messages on LinkedIn and WhatsApp to Indian small-business owners.
2. Posts in niche subreddits like r/India, r/smallbusiness, and r/sidehustle.
3. A one-time lifetime deal on AppSumo or Product Hunt for fast cash.

## Risks & kill-criteria
Risks: Users stop paying after month one; a bigger company copies you; Razorpay or Stripe holds funds; no reason for users to stay.  
Kill criteria: Fewer than 10 new paid users in 60 days, or more than 15 percent of users cancel for two straight months.

## 7-day MVP plan (day-by-day)
MVP means a minimum viable product: the smallest version people will pay for.  
Day 1: Email or call the 10 most active users. Ask what they use the tool for and what almost made them cancel.  
Day 2: List the top three complaints. Pick the easiest one to fix.  
Day 3: Build and ship that fix. Add a "cancel and tell us why" button.  
Day 4: Add annual billing and connect Razorpay plus Stripe.  
Day 5: Get one happy user to write a two-line testimonial with their name and business.  
Day 6: Share that testimonial in the three distribution channels named above.  
Day 7: Count new signups and cancellations. Decide whether to spend money on ads or stop.

## Final call
Build for 30 more days only if you add at least 10 paying users. If growth stalls or churn climbs, kill it and pick a problem you can describe in one sentence.
